# Contributing

Everything goes through the
[Discussions page](https://github.com/bakibektas/builder-kit/discussions). Pick the
category that fits and post your message there.

- **[Q&A](https://github.com/bakibektas/builder-kit/discussions/categories/q-a):** questions,
  and things that went wrong.
- **[Ideas](https://github.com/bakibektas/builder-kit/discussions/categories/ideas):** what
  you would like the kit to do.
- **[Show and tell](https://github.com/bakibektas/builder-kit/discussions/categories/show-and-tell):**
  what you built with it, or how you changed it for yourself.
- **[General](https://github.com/bakibektas/builder-kit/discussions/categories/general):**
  anything else.
